## About Me
Hi, I'm Lucy. She/Her. I make small games and websites. I started coding on Scratch in 2013, and I am proficient in many languages. I also have experience in digital art and graphic design, video and photo editing, 3D and 2D animation, and wiki editing. University of Oregon class of 2026, computer science major and music minor.

I love working on technical projects that involve music. Some of my favorites are The Stoning Music Publication's website, a DJ setlist generator that reads MP3 tags and creates setlists based on BPM and key, a Chrome extension for RateYourMusic with over 70 users, and a MP3 tag scanner that connects to the last.fm API and will tell you which of your most-listened-to songs you have yet to download!

Always learning new skills and languages. I also love backpacking, rock climbing, and reading!
